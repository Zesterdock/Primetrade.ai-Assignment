Project Report: Trader Behavior & Market Sentiment Analysis
1. Methodology

The analysis was conducted by integrating high-frequency trade data from the Hyperliquid DEX with daily Bitcoin Fear & Greed Index values.

    Data Engineering: Standardized timestamps across datasets to a daily grain. Merged ~211,000 trade records with sentiment classifications.

    Metric Calculation: Engineered a suite of behavioral and performance features, including Daily Realized PnL, Win Rate (excluding breakeven trades), Long/Short Ratio, and Average Position Size (USD).

    Segmentation: Traders were categorized using median-based thresholds into four archetypes: Frequent vs. Infrequent (by trade count) and Whale vs. Retail (by average USD size).

    Predictive Modeling: Developed a Random Forest Classifier to forecast next-day PnL Volatility (High vs. Low) based on rolling behavioral metrics and sentiment regimes.

2. Key Insights

    The Fear Premium: Performance significantly improves during "Fear" regimes. Average daily PnL per trader is $2,883 during Fear vs. $2,169 during Greed. Traders show a strong contrarian edge, effectively "buying the blood."

    Directional Bias Shift: The Long/Short ratio remains balanced (~1.26x) during Greed but skyrockets to 6.8x during Fear. This confirms that the Hyperliquid cohort acts as a liquidity provider against market panic.

    The Overtrading Trap: During "Greed" periods, Infrequent traders achieve an average PnL per trade of $155, while Frequent traders drop to $41. High trade frequency during euphoria leads to significant edge dilution and FOMO-driven losses.

    Predictability of Volatility: Behavioral surges (trade count and size) combined with sentiment extremes are highly predictive of risk. The bonus model achieved 93.75% accuracy in forecasting next-day PnL volatility.

3. Strategy Recommendations

Based on the empirical findings, the following "Rules of Thumb" are proposed for the Primetrade.ai ecosystem:

    Rule 1: The "Neutral" Chop Filter (For Retail/Smaller Sizes)

        Observation: Retail traders see their PnL per trade collapse by over 65% (to just $9) during "Neutral" sentiment days.

        Action: Implement a "Risk-Off" filter during Neutral regimes. Reduce position sizes or pause automated retail-targeted strategies when the market lacks a clear psychological trend (Fear/Greed).

    Rule 2: Selective Liquidity Provision (For High-Frequency Segments)

        Observation: Frequent traders underperform significantly during Greed but maintain high activity.

        Action: Enforce a "Trade Cap" or frequency limit during Greed regimes. Pivot frequent strategies to prioritize "Fear" regimes where the win rate and PnL per trade are statistically higher.
